# Cloud & Deployment Glossary

## RTO
Recovery Time Objective: target maximum time to restore service after disruption.

## RPO
Recovery Point Objective: target maximum amount of data loss expressed as time.

## CI
Continuous Integration: automated checks on changes to the codebase.

## CD
Continuous Delivery or Continuous Deployment, depending on context; state which meaning is intended.

## CDN
Content Delivery Network: distributed infrastructure that serves or caches content near users.

## IaC
Infrastructure as Code: declarative or programmatic management of infrastructure configuration.

## SLO
Service Level Objective: a target for a measured reliability indicator.

## SLI
Service Level Indicator: a measurement used to evaluate service behavior.

## PITR
Point-in-time recovery: restore data to a chosen point within a supported recovery window.

## Egress
Traffic leaving a network or service boundary.

## Ingress
Traffic entering a network or service boundary.

## Artifact digest
Content-derived identifier for a specific artifact; for OCI images, a digest identifies the manifest or index content.

## Blue-green deployment
Two deployment environments where traffic can switch from the current environment to a prepared replacement.

## Canary deployment
A rollout that exposes a limited portion of traffic to a new version before wider release.
